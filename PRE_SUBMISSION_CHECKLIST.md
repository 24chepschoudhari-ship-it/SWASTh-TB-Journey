# Final pre-submission checklist

## Game checks

- [ ] Open the deployed GitHub Pages URL, not just the local file.
- [ ] Play Patient, Healthcare Worker and Family / Community at least once.
- [ ] Try different choices within each role; check the feedback, points awarded and next scene.
- [ ] Complete a role and confirm the final score shows earned points, maximum possible points and a percentage.
- [ ] Review whether the score assigned to each option matches the explanation and does not shame the player.
- [ ] Confirm the biology sequence ends with the proposed PstS-1 → C8Nb → TAZ / Tar-EnvZ signalling → LacZ → X-gal → blue readout.
- [ ] Confirm replay, choose-another-role, return-home and choice-review controls work.
- [ ] Switch between English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati. The menus/interface are translated; story scenes are currently English.
- [ ] Test keyboard-only navigation and narrow mobile screens (at least 320 px and 375 px wide).
- [ ] Check that the new score card looks right and remains readable on desktop and mobile.
