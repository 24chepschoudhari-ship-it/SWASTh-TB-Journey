# Sources

These public sources are provided for the general TB information in the game. They are background reading, not evidence that the SWASTh-TB biosensor has been clinically validated.

- World Health Organization, **Tuberculosis fact sheet**: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis
- World Health Organization, **Diagnostic testing for TB**: https://www.who.int/teams/global-programme-on-tuberculosis-and-lung-health/tb-reports/global-tuberculosis-report-2025/tb-diagnosis-and-treatment/2-2-diagnostic-testing
- World Health Organization, **Consolidated guidelines on tuberculosis: Module 3 — Diagnosis**: https://www.who.int/publications/b/58840

The game is educational and is not a diagnosis or individual medical advice. The SWASTh-TB sensing concept shown in the game is proposed research, not a clinically validated diagnostic. Please check the links and wording before final submission.
