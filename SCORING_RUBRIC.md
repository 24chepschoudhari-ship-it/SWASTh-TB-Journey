# Scoring rubric

The score is a reflection feature for this educational game. It is not a medical assessment and should not be used to judge a real patient, family member, or healthcare worker.

Each choice is assigned a score based on the situation in that scene:

- **3 points:** the choice is the most helpful next step in the situation and supports appropriate care, clear information, privacy, or follow-up.
- **2 points:** the choice may help but does not address everything that matters.
- **1 point:** the choice offers limited help or leaves important needs unresolved.
- **0 points:** the choice could contribute to delay, misinformation, missed follow-up, unsupported diagnosis, or stigma.

The points are assigned to individual choices in `game.js`. At the end, the game adds up the points earned and shows them against the maximum possible for the decisions made on that route. It also shows a percentage and a short reflection message.

The result is meant to help players think about their decisions, not to label anyone as good or bad. The team should review the points and explanations together before publishing. They are educational judgements, not a clinically validated measure.
